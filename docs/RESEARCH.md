# Research Interests

The Future Energy Lab focuses on **Artificial Intelligence (AI), Deep Learning, and
data-driven algorithms for future energy and power systems**. Current research activities:

## AI and deep learning for power and energy systems
Physics-informed and data-driven models for detection, classification, forecasting, and
optimization tasks across generation, transmission, and distribution.

## Management and operation of distribution networks
Topology inference, state estimation, and operational decision support for low- and
medium-voltage networks, including validation against field measurements.

## Arc-fault detection
Experimental datasets and machine-learning models for series and parallel arc faults on
residential loads; benchmarking against AFCI devices; physics-informed detection.

## Advanced metering infrastructure and smart metering
Appliance-level power data acquisition, non-intrusive load monitoring (NILM), and anomaly
detection such as electricity-theft identification in residential smart grids.

## Data-center energy systems
Energy modelling and management for data centers, including integration with local
microgrids.

## EV charging management
Coordinated and demand-aware charging strategies for electric-vehicle fleets and chargers.

## Port electrification
Electrical infrastructure and energy management for shore power and port operations.

## Renewable-energy curtailment and hosting capacity
Assessment of network hosting capacity and strategies to minimize renewable-energy
curtailment.
